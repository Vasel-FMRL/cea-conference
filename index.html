About the conference

Early results, open discussion, real connections

CEA is a meeting place for preliminary findings. Much of the work presented has not been published yet, so you hear about it first and can shape it while it is still in progress.

The conference is organized by the Turbulence and Energy Laboratory (TEL) at the University of Windsor, together with partners at the University of South Florida and other institutions. Each year brings researchers, students and industry together to share ideas on energy, the atmosphere, water and the environment.

Students compete for several presentation and poster awards. Judges and attendees both give feedback, and everyone at the event can rate talks through a link shared on the day.

    Keynote speakers

    2027 keynotes

    Dr. Graham Reader

    University of Windsor · BA, BTech (Hons), PhD, psc, FEC, PEng, CEng, CMarE, Eur Ing, FIMarEst

    Engineers and Engineering Contributions to a “Promising Future”

    International declarations on sustainability and resilience are full of ambition, but often light on what can actually be built. This talk asks how engineers can turn that rhetoric into practical, feasible action.

    Dr. Ida-Maja Hassellöv

    Marine chemist · Coordinating author, shipping chapter, third UN World Ocean Assessment

    Wake up! A call for holistic environmental assessment of shipping

    Shipping produces about 3% of human CO₂ emissions, but its other pressures, such as copper from antifouling paint and scrubber discharge, get far less attention. Dr. Hassellöv makes the case for assessing whole ships and whole sea areas together.

    Guidelines

    How to submit

    Submit online in a few minutes. All papers go through blind review.

    1. Prepare your file

      Use the 2027 paper template for full papers. Abstracts can be pasted straight into the form.

    2. Submit online

      Fill in the submission form below and attach your file. It goes straight to the program committee.

    3. Get a confirmation

      You’ll receive a confirmation email right away. If it doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder or write to TELab@uwindsor.ca.

    4. Present in June

      Accepted work is presented orally or as a poster. Selected full papers go to the Springer proceedings.

    Paper template 2027 DOC Manuscript review form DOCX

    Abstract only or full paper

    • Abstract only: you present orally, but the work is not included in the proceedings, books or special issues.
    • Full paper: reviewed for inclusion in the Springer proceedings.
    • Meet each deadline in the important dates.

    Student poster session

    • Open to teams with at least one student author.
    • Choose “Student poster” as the submission type in the form.
    • A work can be a poster or a talk. If you do both, the content must be different.

    Please use the manuscript review form above. A paper should be accepted when it meets these standards:

    • It follows the conference template.
    • The topic is of interest to attendees.
    • It makes a clear and significant contribution.
    • It is written in clear English.
    • It presents new or novel methods or applications, or new or significantly improved processes, designs, technologies, policies or procedures.
    • Arrive early and meet each presenter before your session.
    • Collect presentation slides before the break.
    • Introduce yourself and each speaker.
    • Keep every talk on time.
    • Moderate questions and answers.
    • Close by thanking the speakers and announcing the next session.
    Want to organize a session? Send your session proposal to TELab@uwindsor.ca.
